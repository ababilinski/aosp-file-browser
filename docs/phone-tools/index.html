Do more from your Mac.

Files and storage

Find files. See what’s taking up space.

Search by name, type, or date, and see what’s using space on your phone.

Apps

Install and manage apps.

Installing apps is as easy as drag and drop. Open, stop, or remove them right from your Mac.

Trash

Deleted something? Bring it back.

Restore files deleted in AOSP File Manager, or remove them for good.

Capture and control

Use your phone from your Mac.

Open apps, tap, scroll, and type without picking up your phone.

  • Take screenshots.

    Capture one screen—or several—in a single image.

  • Record your screen.

    Record one screen—or several—in a single video.

  • Control your phone.

    View and control your Android phone from your Mac.

  • Control more than one phone.

    Open each connected phone in its own window, with controls for navigation, volume, rotation, screenshots, and power.
